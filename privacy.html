Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. We keep our practices straightforward.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Information we collect

Evergreen Digital Media does not require visitors to create an account, and we do not maintain a database of website visitors. If you contact us through our contact form, we receive the name, email address, and other information you choose to submit, solely to respond to your inquiry.

Website analytics and cookies

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand general site traffic. Google Analytics may use cookies and collect technical information about visits, such as browser details, pages visited, and approximate location. We do not use this information to identify individual visitors.

Third-party services

Our contact form is processed by Formspree, and our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. These providers may process information necessary to provide their services under their own privacy practices. Links to YouTube and other external websites are subject to those sites' privacy policies.

Sharing and selling information

We do not sell personal information, share contact submissions for marketing, or use submitted contact information to build advertising lists. Information may be handled by the service providers described above to operate this website and respond to inquiries.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or information you have submitted, please use our contact form.
